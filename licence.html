Family Dashboard – Licence agreement

Version 1 · 14 November 2026

This is the deal between you and the seller named in your licence email (“we”). By adding your licence key, you agree to it.

1. What you’re buying

Permission to use the Family Dashboard software in your own home. You pay once, and there’s no end date.

2. What your key covers

  • One family dashboard – one family Google Sheet.
  • The number of screens in your licence email. Phones, and the apps on them, don’t count.
  • You can move your key to a new Sheet or Google account (Configurator → Software Licence).

3. Updates

Updates and fixes are included for as long as we support the software. They only go to dashboards with an active licence key.

4. Please don’t

  • share, sell or give away your key;
  • copy the software for other people or households;
  • change the software to get around the licence check.

If you do, we may switch your key off.

5. Your family’s information

It stays in your own Google account, and we never see it. The licence check only sends your key, a random installation number and the software version. There’s more in the privacy notice.

6. Other services

The dashboard needs a Google account, and some widgets use free outside services such as weather, travel and news. They can change or stop, which is outside our control. A few widgets work in the UK only.

7. If something goes wrong

Tell us and we’ll do our best to put it right. Please don’t rely on the dashboard alone for anything important, like medicines or safety. Nothing here affects your legal rights as a consumer.

8. The law

The law of England and Wales applies. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you can also use your local courts.
