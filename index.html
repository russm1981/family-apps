HouseRunnera family dashboard❓ Help

Your family’s week, at a glance

HouseRunner turns a small screen – like the Corsair XENEON EDGE, a tablet or an old monitor – into your family’s dashboard: the calendar, weather, bins, the school run, reminders and the children’s reward points, all in one place. Two phone apps keep it up to date.

Start new setup

Setup takes about 30 minutes, one step at a time. Starting a new setup makes a new Google Sheet – any earlier setups stay in your Google account, unaffected.

What it does

📅One family calendar

Everyone’s plans in their own colour, from your Google calendars – on the screen and on everyone’s phone.

⭐Rewards for the children

Points for jobs and good days, Daily To-Dos, and their own card on the screen.

🌦Before you leave

Weather, traffic to the places you go, trains and buses – at a glance.

🎒Never forget

Bin days, sports kit, library books and the other things each day needs.

📌Notes and lists

Leave a note on the screen from your phone; shopping and to-do lists everyone can add to.

🔒Private by design

Your family’s information stays in your own Google account. We never see it.

How it works

1Your own Google Sheet

Setup makes a private Google Sheet in your Google account. It holds everything – only you can see it.

2Connect your screen

The Corsair XENEON EDGE on a Windows PC, a tablet, a Raspberry Pi or any computer. It shows a code – type it in and it’s connected.

3Add the phone apps

Family Admin for the grown-ups (points, lists, settings) and Family Dashboard – the dashboard itself – for everyone. Scan a QR code and add them to your home screen.

What you need

  • A Google account – ideally the one your family calendar is in. Make one free
  • A computer for setup – a Windows PC or a Mac
  • A display for the dashboard – the Corsair XENEON EDGE, a tablet, a Raspberry Pi with a monitor, or any computer
  • A phone for the grown-ups’ app
  • Your licence key – it’s in your order email (setup fills it in for you from the email’s link)
