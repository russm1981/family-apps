Family Dashboard

Two phone apps that work with the dashboard on the XENEON EDGE.

Family CalendarFor everyone – see and add entries ⚙Set up a new dashboardStep-by-step first-time setup Family AdminFor parents – points, tasks, lists, settings

Install on your phone

  1. iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open the app in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open it from the new icon and paste your setup link when asked.
